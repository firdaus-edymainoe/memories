Your files already live on a disk.

Memories is a file manager for this computer, the phone, the SSD, and the USB in the drawer. A drive is a folder you choose. Backup copies onto a disk you trust. Cloud is a spare you can add later.

Open the mock See how it works

It runs on this computer. Not in a browser tab.

A website cannot see a USB stick plug in, or an iPhone Camera Roll. Memories lives next to Finder, so your disks are first-class.

1

Choose a folder as a drive

This Mac’s Photos folder. Camera on the phone. A folder on an SSD. Memories remembers what’s in it. The files stay put.

2

See them clearly

Images, videos, documents — by date. Photos and videos also as events, grouped by day and place.

3

Backup is a separate step

Open folders on a drive, check the ones to copy, then pick a disk. Plug both in, then Start. Originals stay where they are.

Use it to tidy the disks in the house. Pay only if you want offsite.

Free

On your computer

Choose a folder on This Mac, the phone, the SSD, the travel USB. Browse, back up between those drives, and keep working with the network unplugged. No account required.
