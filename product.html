Memories

Your family’s files, already sorted for you.

Photos, videos, school letters and receipts — from this computer, your phone and the drives in the drawer — in one calm place. No folders to keep tidy.

Try the preview Why it helps

Looking for something shouldn’t mean digging through folders.

Most of us start with neat folders, then life gets busy. Memories sorts for you instead — so finding a file feels like asking a simple question.

“I’m looking for a photo…”

See by type

Photos together. Videos together. Documents by kind — PDF, Word, spreadsheets. Open the format you need.

“I’m looking for that weekend…”

See by date

Flip to Date and jump to the month or day it happened. Same files, another way home.

“Where did we put the spare copy?”

Keep a spare

Backup copies chosen folders onto another drive you trust. Your originals never move.

Type — find the kind of file Date — find when it happened Moments — days that belong together

Three quiet steps. Then you’re home.

Nothing gets uploaded for you. Files stay where they already are — Memories just helps you see them clearly.

The wedding lives on five places. Open it as one day.

Aisha isn’t hunting folders named Final_final_2. She opens Memories, finds that Saturday in Kuala Lumpur, and sees every photo — phone, travel stick, Summer drive. If a drive is in the drawer, it’s still listed. Plug it in, and those files open.
